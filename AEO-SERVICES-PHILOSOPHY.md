# AEO Services — Philosophy

Working note, started 2026-11-17. This is the general philosophy behind a
service Desk Agent will eventually offer under Rashad's portfolio, and the
seed of future marketing around it. Not a live offer yet — Jath Shao (back
office + law social) comes first; LovingMindsCare (psych, Google-first) second.

## The shift: from links to answers

Search is becoming answers. People used to get ten blue links; now they get
one answer from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, or a Google AI Overview — and that
answer cites a handful of sources. Ranking #1 matters less than being the
source the answer trusts.

- **SEO** (Search Engine Optimization): be found in search results.
- **AEO** (Answer Engine Optimization, also called **GEO**, Generative Engine
  Optimization): be cited and recommended *inside* AI answers.
- AEO is not a separate magic product. It is SEO + structured, authoritative
  content + reputation, repackaged for machines that answer instead of list.
  The inputs are unglamorous: accurate business data, real reviews, FAQ and
  explainer pages with schema markup, and content an AI can quote without
  embarrassment.

## Loop engineering: the service is a loop, not a deliverable

Everybody will sell SEO/AEO — it is already crowded. The bottleneck is the
pile-up of work: profile, reviews, content, listings, week after week.
Agencies sell the audit and stop. The moat is *running the loop* while
others sell the setup.

A loop is: **measure → diagnose → act → re-measure.** The client pays for
the loop running, not for a report. Each loop has one owner metric, one
cadence, and one action it triggers.

## The four loops (top metrics)

| # | Loop / metric | Cadence | What it triggers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Booked business from Google** — calls, form fills, bookings attributed to GBP/Ads/AI answers; cost per booked appointment or client | Monthly deep check | The dollar metric. If this is flat, everything else is vanity. Drives budget and content priorities. |
| 2 | **Local visibility** — GBP search impressions, calls, direction requests, website clicks; Maps ranking for money keywords | Weekly light check | Profile fixes, new posts, Q&A, hours/services updates. |
| 3 | **AI citation share** — how often the business is cited or recommended in AI answers for target queries ("psychiatrist near me", "immigration lawyer Miami") | Monthly | New FAQ/explainer pages, schema fixes, authority gaps to close. |
| 4 | **Reputation** — review count, average rating, response rate and response time | Weekly | Review requests, replies, and any service issue a review reveals. |

Supporting checks (monthly): organic rankings and traffic for money pages,
ad spend efficiency, content freshness (new pages indexed and cited).

**Cadence:** weekly light loop (reviews, profile insights, ad spend) →
monthly deep loop (rankings, AI citations, booked business, content gaps →
next month's work) → quarterly strategy (new pages, new markets, new offers).

## How the service is sold

- **Separate from back office.** Back office ties to dollars (clients booked,
  hours saved). Visibility ties to booked business too — loop #1 keeps it
  honest — but it is a separate line with a separate price.
- **Client pays their own tools and ad spend.** Desk Agent charges for the
  work, never funds the client's stack out of its fee.
- **Stitch, don't build.** Twilio, Zapier, Google tools, Gemini — no custom
  payroll, no custom APIs, no custom orchestration in Phase 1. Google Drive
  is the database at one-person scale; a real database comes when there are
  multiple clients or real insights work.
- **Approval-gated.** Nothing publishes, replies, or spends without the
  client's approval of the exact copy or campaign.
- **Trial, then review.** Three months, measured on booked business, then
  decide together.

## What changes by vertical

- **Law (immigration):** story-based and discussion-driven — social *and*
  Google. The attorney's own story is the engine; social converts slowly,
  Google converts on intent. Attorney advertising rules apply: no outcome
  promises, proper disclaimers.
- **Psychiatric services:** Google-first, not social. No patient stories,
  HIPAA everywhere, Google restricts ads and remarketing around mental-health
  conditions. Education and awareness only. Health content faces the highest
  trust bar (YMYL: experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trust).
- **Trades (Boss Man playbook):** the same loops, pointed at missed calls and
  booked jobs — the model this philosophy was learned on.

## What we never do

- No patient/client health information in any AI or ad tool. Ever.
- No legal or medical advice in automated replies or posts. Education only;
  every question routes to a paid, time-boxed consultation.
- No outcome promises, no fake reviews, no misleading claims.
- No custom software for a problem a $50 tool already solves.
